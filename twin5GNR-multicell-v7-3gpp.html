5G NR Multicell Twin v7 — 3GPP only

Map: San Francisco 1 km × 1 km, downtown tile (centre 37.801105, −122.404175), 3 towers × 3 sectors. Model: 3GPP TR 38.901 (modelled) — computed live on CPU. All figures are modelled, never measured. This is a 3GPP-only rebuild of v7: the Sionna and Sionna + trees modes of v7 are not included — they are ray-traced data that need a GPU retrace and cannot be recomputed here. Move a tower and press Run: the map, the coverage figures, the median RSRP and the handoff region all recompute. Version inside the file: v7-3gpp.

Drag a tower marker (▲) on the map to move it — the position boxes update live; release to recompute. One tower at a time; the others do not move. Dragging clears the preset (custom position). For exact positions, type the numbers instead, then press Run.

How to read this: Colour = best server (strongest RSRP, modelled) at that point. White dots = handoff region, where the best and second-best cells are within 1 CQI of each other — a UE there would normally hand over, and ping-pong is possible. The trigger is the UE-reported CQI (quantised SINR, 0-15): the UE measures SINR on every sector, maps it to CQI, and reports it; the network hands over when a neighbour's CQI beats the serving cell's CQI. CQI ≥ 1 floor (applied): CQI 0 means out of range / no usable link, and CQI 0 cells do not count as handoff candidates. SINR here is the real one — serving RSRP against all other sectors' RSRP plus thermal noise — not the old 3 dB-below-serving assumption. Triangle = tower. Dot = UE, with its label. RSRP = EIRP − path loss, modelled. Capacity = bandwidth × log2(1+SINR) × MIMO streams, capped at the MIMO order, modelled, not measured throughput. Doppler = speed × frequency / speed of light, shown per UE. Morphology changes path loss only; it does not change the map shape.

Assumptions, stated: 3GPP TR 38.901 simplified LOS path loss, with 3D distance: UMa 28.0 + 22·log10(d) + 20·log10(f); UMi 32.4 + 21·log10(d) + 20·log10(f); RMa 20·log10(d) + 20·log10(f) + 20 approx., d = √(d₂D² + (h_tower − 1.5)²) in metres (UE height 1.5 m, street level), f in GHz, minimum distance 10 m. The v7 source used 2D distance only and stated no tower height; here heights enter through the 3D distance, which is the TR 38.901 form. As-built tower height is not stated in the v7 source — the 25 m default is assumed (3GPP UMa typical), labelled as assumed. Noise −174 dBm/Hz, noise figure 7 dB, SINR is computed per sector from all sectors' RSRP plus thermal noise; the UE reports it as CQI (0-15, ~2 dB steps from -6 dB, stated approx.), and handover is decided on CQI with the CQI ≥ 1 floor above. ICI degradation, calculated (not assumed as a flat factor): ICI ratio = (π·f_D / Δf)² / 3, where f_D is Doppler and Δf is subcarrier spacing (15 kHz at 0.7 GHz, 30 kHz at 3.5/3.6 GHz, 60 kHz at 7.125 GHz, 120 kHz at 28 GHz, stated). Effective SINR = 1 / (1/SINR + ICI ratio), for both DL and UL. DL uses tower EIRP and selected MIMO; UL uses UE power 23 dBm and MIMO capped at 2, so UL is usually lower. Antenna pattern is directional 65° per sector: gain 15 dBi at boresight, falling as 12·(angle/65)², capped at 25 dB down, per 3GPP TR 38.901 simplified pattern. Each tower has 3 sectors at 0°, 120° and 240°. UEs move in straight lines and bounce at the grid edge. This is a teaching / planning sketch, not a site survey. Coverage note: in this 3GPP UMa model at EIRP 47 dBm the 1 km tile is fully covered at RSRP ≥ −90 dBm for any tower position inside the tile, so that figure stays 100.0% when a tower moves — the move shows up in the map, the median RSRP, the handoff region, and the strong-coverage (≥ −70 dBm) share below.
Shifted-site presets — lat/lon to grid conversion (WGS84, tile centre 37.801105, −122.404175): 1° latitude = 110,992.7 m; 1° longitude = 88,069.2 m. Grid x = 500 + (lon − lon₀)·88,069.2; grid y = 500 + (lat₀ − lat)·110,992.7 (y grows southward). As-built from the CSV: A (249.7, 250.7), B (750.3, 250.7), C (500.0, 749.3) — within 1 m of the v7 defaults (250, 250), (750, 250), (500, 750); preset grid values are rounded to the nearest metre. Applying a preset moves that one tower to its shifted position and height and resets the other two towers to as-built (25 m assumed). v7 source reference figures (3GPP, 3.5 GHz): coverage ≥ −90 dBm 100.0%, median best-server RSRP −31.9 dBm; floored handoff region 12.6% (deck figure).
