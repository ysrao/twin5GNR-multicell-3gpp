How to read this: Colour = best server (strongest RSRP) at that point. White dots = handoff region for the selected UE, where the best and second-best cells are within its mobility threshold — a UE there would normally hand over, and ping-pong is possible. Thresholds, modelled and stated: static/pedestrian 3 dB, cyclist 4 dB, car 30 km/h 5 dB, car 60 km/h 6 dB, high-speed 120 km/h 8 dB. Faster UEs get a wider handoff region, because they travel further during time-to-trigger and need an earlier handover to avoid a drop. The 'All UEs' map uses the static 3 dB threshold only, for comparison. Triangle = tower. Dot = UE, with its label. RSRP = EIRP − path loss, modelled. Capacity = bandwidth × log2(1+SINR) × MIMO streams, capped at the MIMO order, modelled, not measured throughput. Doppler = speed × frequency / speed of light, shown per UE. Morphology changes path loss only; it does not change the map shape.
3GPP vs Sionna — what the toggle shows (ray-traced / simulated, 3.6 GHz, street 1.5 m, 625 cells at 40 m):
  • Coverage (RSRP ≥ −90 dBm): Sionna 80.5% of cells vs 3GPP UMa 100.0% — the ray-traced map covers less, because buildings shadow whole street blocks that the formula treats as open.
  • Median best-server RSRP: Sionna -72.7 dBm vs 3GPP -31.9 dBm (median difference Sionna − 3GPP -40.9 dB).
  • Building shadow: 79.7% of cells are >15 dB weaker in Sionna than in 3GPP; 10.1% of cells receive no ray at all from any tower (hatched). LOS to at least one tower: 23.7% of cells.
  • Best server: the two models pick the same serving tower on 73.6% of cells — where they disagree is mostly at tower boundaries and in shadowed streets.
  • Handoff region (<3 dB, sector level): Sionna 3.4% of cells vs 3GPP 20.5% — the ray-traced handoff region is patchier: it follows street canyons instead of smooth circles, so a moving UE can cross several handoff boundaries where the 3GPP map shows one.
Assumptions, stated: Sionna sectors 90°/210°/330° in ENU (as in the SF single-tower run); 3GPP sectors 0°/120°/240° in artefact grid coordinates. Best-server comparison is per tower, not per sector. Sionna is a simulation on a 1 m-facet mesh with one material (ITU concrete) for the whole city — it is not a measurement, and it does not include vegetation, vehicles, or indoor loss.

Noise −174 dBm/Hz, noise figure 7 dB, interference modelled as 3 dB below serving RSRP for SINR. ICI degradation in v4, calculated (not assumed as a flat factor): ICI ratio = (π·f_D / Δf)² / 3, where f_D is Doppler and Δf is subcarrier spacing (15 kHz at 0.7 GHz, 30 kHz at 3.5 GHz, 60 kHz at 7.125 GHz, 120 kHz at 28 GHz, stated). Effective SINR = 1 / (1/SINR + ICI ratio), for both DL and UL. DL uses tower EIRP and selected MIMO; UL uses UE power 23 dBm and MIMO capped at 2, so UL is usually lower. Antenna pattern in v3 is directional 65° per sector: gain 15 dBi at boresight, falling as 12·(angle/65)², capped at 25 dB down, per 3GPP TR 38.901 simplified pattern. Each tower has 3 sectors at 0°, 120° and 240°. UEs move in straight lines and bounce at the grid edge. This is a teaching / planning sketch, not a site survey.
