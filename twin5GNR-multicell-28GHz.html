Cells with no ray to any tower are shown hatched (no coverage). Cell locations are fixed in v8 (the 16 ray-traced small-cell positions).

Sionna + trees mode: same ray-traced RSRP as Sionna mode, with ITU-R P.833-10 foliage loss applied per small-cell–receiver path from the 1,919 surveyed DataSF street trees (exact positions; crown size assumed from trunk diameter, P.833 (gamma, Am) = (2.20, 50) at 28 GHz interpolated/assumed, uncalibrated). Foliage depth is the straight-line path length inside tree crowns, sampled every 5 m. At 28 GHz the effect is large — see the comparison note — because the low 8 m cells put the ray inside the crown band for most of its path. Free 3DEP LiDAR confirms 97.6% of the surveyed trees within 2 m, but its canopy-height layer cannot be used as the headline foliage layer: it has no crown bottom and counts clutter and unmapped buildings, overstating foliage depth ~10× versus the surveyed positions.

Fixed-band data: Frequency is fixed at 28 GHz in this version. In Sionna modes, Frequency, Morphology and MIMO apply to the 3GPP model only — Sionna data are fixed at 28 GHz.

How to read this: Colour = best server (strongest RSRP) at that point. White dots = handoff region, where the best and second-best cells are within 1 CQI of each other — a UE there would normally hand over, and ping-pong is possible. The trigger is the UE-reported CQI (quantised SINR, 0-15): the UE measures SINR on every sector, maps it to CQI, and reports it; the network hands over when a neighbour's CQI beats the serving cell's CQI. SINR here is the real one — serving RSRP against all other sectors' RSRP plus thermal noise — not the old 3 dB-below-serving assumption. Triangle = tower. Dot = UE, with its label. RSRP = EIRP − path loss, modelled. Capacity = bandwidth × log2(1+SINR) × MIMO streams, capped at the MIMO order, modelled, not measured throughput. Doppler = speed × frequency / speed of light, shown per UE. Morphology changes path loss only; it does not change the map shape.
3GPP UMi vs Sionna vs Sionna + trees — 28 GHz small cells (all modes 28 GHz only; ray-traced / simulated, street 1.5 m, 625 cells at 40 m, 16 cells at 8 m AGL, EIRP 40 dBm):
  • Coverage (RSRP ≥ −90 dBm): 3GPP UMi 100.0% vs Sionna 32.6% vs Sionna + trees 29.9%.
  • Median best-server RSRP: 3GPP UMi -50.9 dBm vs Sionna -97.4 dBm vs Sionna + trees -98.1 dBm (Sionna − 3GPP UMi median -46.9 dB). LOS to at least one small cell: 19.4% of cells; no ray at all: 16.6%.
  • Where trees bite at 28 GHz: median change +0.0 dB, mean -1.35 dB; 7.2% of cells lose more than 1 dB to foliage; worst cell loses -41.4 dB. Best server changes on 2.2% of cells. Handoff region (<3 dB, sector level): 3GPP UMi 35.2% vs Sionna 13.8% vs Sionna + trees 15.0%.
  • Why trees matter here and not at 3.6 GHz: the 8 m cells put the ray inside the tree-crown band (2–12 m) for most of its 250 m path, so mean foliage depth is 20.46 m (vs 4.07 m for the 30 m macros at 3.6 GHz), and P.833 at 28 GHz has (gamma, Am) = (2.20, 50), so the mean foliage loss is 24.59 dB. This matches the Nov 5 surveyed-tree verdict: vegetation matters at 28 GHz, street level, 100–500 m.
  • LiDAR sensitivity (not in the toggle): the free USGS 3DEP canopy-height layer gives mean foliage depth 142.23 m (mean P.833 loss 46.51 dB, saturating at 50 dB) — an upper bound, because it has no crown bottom and counts clutter and unmapped buildings. Its median RSRP change is +0.0 dB, mean -3.47 dB. The toggle uses the surveyed trees only; the LiDAR layer is reported here, not embedded.
Assumptions, stated: 16 small cells on a 4×4 grid, 250 m spacing, 8 m AGL (lamppost assumption); 3 sectors per cell (90°/210°/330° in ENU, as in v5 — real lamppost cells are often omni); 4×4 array (real 28 GHz arrays are often 8×8); EIRP 40 dBm; Sionna sectors as in v5; 3GPP sectors 0°/120°/240° in artefact grid coordinates. Foliage: P.833-10 Eq.(1), (gamma, Am) = (2.20, 50) at 28 GHz interpolated/assumed and uncalibrated; crown size assumed from trunk diameter; phi-weighted (LOS share 1 for LOS pairs, 0 for non-LOS). Sionna is a simulation on a 1 m-facet mesh with one material (ITU concrete) — it is not a measurement, and it does not include vehicles or indoor loss. The 3GPP UMi baseline is LOS-only (no shadowing), so it is an upper bound; UMa LOS is also computed in the comparison file.

Noise −174 dBm/Hz, noise figure 7 dB, SINR is computed per sector from all sectors' RSRP plus thermal noise; the UE reports it as CQI (0-15, ~2 dB steps from -6 dB, stated approx.), and handover is decided on CQI. ICI degradation in v4, calculated (not assumed as a flat factor): ICI ratio = (π·f_D / Δf)² / 3, where f_D is Doppler and Δf is subcarrier spacing (15 kHz at 0.7 GHz, 30 kHz at 3.5 GHz, 60 kHz at 7.125 GHz, 120 kHz at 28 GHz, stated). Effective SINR = 1 / (1/SINR + ICI ratio), for both DL and UL. DL uses tower EIRP and selected MIMO; UL uses UE power 23 dBm and MIMO capped at 2, so UL is usually lower. Antenna pattern in v3 is directional 65° per sector: gain 15 dBi at boresight, falling as 12·(angle/65)², capped at 25 dB down, per 3GPP TR 38.901 simplified pattern. Each tower has 3 sectors at 0°, 120° and 240°. UEs move in straight lines and bounce at the grid edge. This is a teaching / planning sketch, not a site survey.
