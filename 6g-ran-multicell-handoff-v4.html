How to read this: Colour = best server (strongest RSRP) at that point. White dots = handoff region for the selected UE, where the best and second-best cells are within its mobility threshold — a UE there would normally hand over, and ping-pong is possible. Thresholds, modelled and stated: static/pedestrian 3 dB, cyclist 4 dB, car 30 km/h 5 dB, car 60 km/h 6 dB, high-speed 120 km/h 8 dB. Faster UEs get a wider handoff region, because they travel further during time-to-trigger and need an earlier handover to avoid a drop. The 'All UEs' map uses the static 3 dB threshold only, for comparison. Triangle = tower. Dot = UE, with its label. RSRP = EIRP − path loss, modelled. Capacity = bandwidth × log2(1+SINR) × MIMO streams, capped at the MIMO order, modelled, not measured throughput. Doppler = speed × frequency / speed of light, shown per UE. Morphology changes path loss only; it does not change the map shape.

Assumptions, stated: 3GPP TR 38.901 simplified LOS path loss: UMa 28.0 + 22·log10(d) + 20·log10(f); UMi 32.4 + 21·log10(d) + 20·log10(f); RMa 20·log10(d) + 20·log10(f) + 20 approx., d in metres, f in GHz, minimum distance 10 m. Noise −174 dBm/Hz, noise figure 7 dB, interference modelled as 3 dB below serving RSRP for SINR. ICI degradation in v4, calculated (not assumed as a flat factor): ICI ratio = (π·f_D / Δf)² / 3, where f_D is Doppler and Δf is subcarrier spacing (15 kHz at 0.7 GHz, 30 kHz at 3.5 GHz, 60 kHz at 7.125 GHz, 120 kHz at 28 GHz, stated). Effective SINR = 1 / (1/SINR + ICI ratio), for both DL and UL. DL uses tower EIRP and selected MIMO; UL uses UE power 23 dBm and MIMO capped at 2, so UL is usually lower. Antenna pattern in v3 is directional 65° per sector: gain 15 dBi at boresight, falling as 12·(angle/65)², capped at 25 dB down, per 3GPP TR 38.901 simplified pattern. Each tower has 3 sectors at 0°, 120° and 240°. UEs move in straight lines and bounce at the grid edge. This is a teaching / planning sketch, not a site survey.
